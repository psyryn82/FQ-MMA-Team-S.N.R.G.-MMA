Refunds & Outages

Last updated October 20, 2026

Payment questions

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, were charged more than once, or paid but did not receive access, keep your receipt and contact us at fqsnrgmma.info@gmail.com. Include the email used at checkout, the event or item name, and the approximate purchase time. Do not email full card numbers or security codes.

Streaming interruptions

Live streams depend on internet connectivity, devices, and third-party delivery services. If playback stops, first refresh the playback page and check your connection. If the problem continues, contact support with your receipt and a description of the interruption.

Refund review

Refund requests are reviewed based on the purchase, access history, reported issue, and applicable law. Contacting support does not automatically approve a refund. Nothing on this page limits any rights you may have under applicable law.

Event changes or cancellations

Event dates, venues, and broadcast availability may change. If an event is postponed, cancelled, or materially affected by an outage, contact support for information about the options available for that purchase.

Contact
