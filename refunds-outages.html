Refund Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This policy applies to purchases made through FQ MMA / Team S.N.R.G. MMA, including live-stream tickets, paid digital photos or videos, and merchandise. Your order confirmation or product listing may include additional item-specific details. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

1. Live-stream tickets

  • Event canceled: If the event is canceled and not rescheduled, we will refund the ticket price to the original payment method, unless you choose another option offered by us.
  • Event postponed or rescheduled: Your ticket will generally remain valid for the rescheduled event. If you cannot attend the new date, contact support promptly so we can review available options.
  • Stream access problem: If you paid but cannot access the stream, contact us as soon as possible. We will first try to resolve the access issue. If a substantial part of the purchased live stream is unavailable due to a problem within our control and cannot reasonably be restored, we will review the circumstances and may offer a full or partial refund or another appropriate remedy.
  • Individual connection or device issues: We cannot guarantee playback on every device or internet connection. Before the event, check your connection and supported device/browser. We will still review genuine access problems individually.
  • Live-only access: If a listing says “live viewing only” or “no replay,” replay or on-demand access is not included unless expressly stated.

2. Digital photos and videos

Because digital files can be delivered and copied, digital downloads are generally non-refundable once the download or access has been delivered. If the file is corrupted, materially different from its description, not delivered, or inaccessible because of a problem on our side, contact support. We will work to correct the problem or provide an appropriate refund where warranted or required by law.

3. Merchandise

For eligible merchandise, contact support within 14 days after delivery before sending anything back. Items must be unused and in their original condition and packaging, unless defective or incorrect. We will provide return instructions. Unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, the customer is responsible for return shipping. After we receive and inspect an approved return, any approved refund will be sent to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to post it. This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights.

4. Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged more than once or the amount is incorrect, contact us with your order receipt and the email used at checkout. Never send full payment-card numbers or security codes by email.

5. How to request help or a refund

Email support@fq-snrg-mma.com. Include your name, order/receipt number if available, the event or item, the email used at checkout, and a description of the issue. For streaming problems, include when the problem occurred and what troubleshooting you tried. We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible.
