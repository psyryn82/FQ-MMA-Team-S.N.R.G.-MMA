Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

This policy describes how FQ MMA / Team S.N.R.G. MMA handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

Contact and booking forms may collect your name, email address, phone number, service preferences, requested dates, and message details. Account features use information needed to authenticate your account. Purchase flows may use your email address to associate a receipt with the item you purchased.

Payments and service providers

Payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not ask you to submit full payment-card details in our website forms. Service providers may support account sign-in, payment processing, email delivery, security checks, and media streaming.

How information is used

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, review booking requests, administer accounts, confirm purchases, deliver access, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain site security.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including support, recordkeeping, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your inquiry or booking. We may need to retain certain records for legitimate business, security, or legal reasons.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact form or to fqsnrgmma.info@gmail.com.
